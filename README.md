# CareerPilot-X – AI-Powered Career Guidance Platform

## Project Description
CareerPilot-X is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students and job seekers make informed career decisions. It provides personalized career recommendations based on users' skills, interests, qualifications, and career goals.

The platform aims to simplify career planning by helping users identify suitable job roles, explore career opportunities, improve their skills, and prepare for future employment.

## Features
- **Personalized Career Recommendations:** Suggests suitable career paths based on users' skills and interests.
- **AI-Powered Guidance:** Provides intelligent career suggestions and assistance.
- **Skill Analysis:** Helps users identify their strengths and areas for improvement.
- **Career Exploration:** Enables users to explore different job roles and career opportunities.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Offers a simple and interactive platform for career planning.

## Objectives
- To provide personalized career guidance using AI.
- To help students identify suitable career paths.
- To bridge the gap between skills and job opportunities.
- To support informed career planning and skill development.
